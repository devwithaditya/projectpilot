About this calculator

Plan the paint
before you buy.

This paint calculator estimates how much interior wall paint you need based on room dimensions, openings, coats, coverage, and waste. You can also include a ceiling and one coat of primer.

What you can estimate

  • Total wall and ceiling area
  • Door and window deductions
  • Gallons to purchase with overage
  • Paint and primer material cost
Before you start

Have these numbers ready

Room length, room width, wall height, number of doors and windows, number of coats, and the coverage printed on your paint can.

Prices are editable examples. Replace them with your local store price for a more useful planning estimate.
