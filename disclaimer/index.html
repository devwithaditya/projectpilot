What ProjectPilot estimates mean—and what they do not replace.

Disclaimer

Last updated: September 10, 2026

ProjectPilot provides general planning tools for homeowners and DIY users. Results are estimates based on the measurements, prices, options, and assumptions entered by the user.

Not a professional quote

ProjectPilot results are not contractor bids, purchase orders, engineering designs, architectural plans, inspections, or guarantees of final cost. Actual quantities and costs can vary due to location, product availability, material quality, labor rates, site conditions, waste, access, taxes, delivery, permits, and project complexity.

Safety and local requirements

ProjectPilot does not determine whether a project is safe, permitted, structurally appropriate, or compliant with local law. Check local codes and requirements, call utility-locating services before digging, follow product instructions, and consult a qualified professional when the work involves structural, electrical, plumbing, gas, roofing, excavation, or other specialized risks.

DIY versus contractor results

The DIY comparison uses the value you assign to your time and the inputs you provide. It cannot assess your skill, health, safety, warranty, quality, schedule, or the complete scope of a contractor’s work.

Use your judgment

Review the assumptions, replace example prices with local information, and confirm important decisions with suppliers and qualified professionals before acting.
