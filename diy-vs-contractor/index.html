About this calculator

Make the trade-off
visible.

DIY can reduce cash spending while increasing time, learning, and uncertainty. This comparison adds the value you assign to your time to the costs you can see on a receipt.

What you can compare

  • DIY materials, tools, and rentals
  • Hours multiplied by your time value
  • Contractor quote and duration
  • Experience-based difficulty context
Keep it balanced

A tool, not professional advice

A lower calculated cost does not guarantee a better outcome. Skill, safety, warranty, permits, schedule, and enjoyment all matter.

Use the result to ask better questions and compare the scope of the quote.
