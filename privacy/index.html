How ProjectPilot handles information when you use the website and its home project calculators.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

This starter privacy policy explains the current ProjectPilot MVP. It should be reviewed and updated before production launch, especially if analytics, advertising, accounts, or third-party services are added.

Information processed by the tools

The calculators are designed to run in your browser. Measurements and prices entered into a calculator are used to produce the result on your device and are not currently sent to a ProjectPilot server or stored in a ProjectPilot account.

Local browser storage

If you dismiss the Android install message, the site may store a small preference in your browser so the message does not immediately return. You can clear this preference through your browser settings.

Contact email

If you email ProjectPilot, we receive the information you choose to include in that message, such as your email address, message, and attachments. We use it to respond and do not sell contact messages.

Third-party services

The current design may request fonts or other static assets from third-party providers. A production deployment should document every analytics, advertising, hosting, consent, or affiliate service that is enabled.

Future changes

This policy may change as ProjectPilot adds features. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new update date.
